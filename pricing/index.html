Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest, straightforward answers about billing, data storage, limits, and how the service works.

Who owns my data, and can I export it?

You own 100% of your data. You can export your entire library at any time in standard formats (HTML bookmarks, CSV, or structured JSON), including all original extracted text and metadata. If you ever leave, your data comes with you.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. You can cancel with one click from your account settings at https://app.keepfor.me/pricing. Your access remains active until the end of your current billing period, with no cancellation penalties or hidden conditions.

What counts as an AI summary or ask query?

An AI summary is counted when you ingest an article and generate a concise structured summary. An ask-your-library query is counted when you ask a natural-language question directly in the app or via your connected MCP client (such as Claude or Cursor) that searches and synthesizes answers across your library.

What happens when I reach my monthly quota?

When you reach your monthly limit, your library continues working normally: you can save articles, read them in reader mode, search using keyword FTS5, and export data. Only new AI summaries and ask queries pause until your next monthly billing cycle resets, or until you upgrade to Pro.

How do trial rules work?

The free trial lasts for 14 days, or until you reach 50 AI summaries or 20 ask-your-library queries, whichever comes first. This gives you plenty of headroom to test reader mode, mobile saving, and Claude or Cursor MCP integrations before committing.

Self-host free vs hosted SaaS: which should I choose?

If you enjoy DevOps, maintain your own Cloudflare account, and prefer configuring your own database and AI keys, the AGPL-3.0 open-source edition is free forever. If you want zero maintenance, automatic backups, instant sync across devices, and included AI compute quotas without managing bindings, choose our hosted SaaS.

Where is data stored and what about privacy?

Your data is stored in dedicated Cloudflare D1 databases and encrypted Cloudflare R2 object storage. We do not sell your personal data, run ad trackers, or use your private bookmarks and notes to train third-party foundation models.

What AI features are included in hosted plans?

AI features (automated summaries, smart tag suggestions, hybrid vector semantic search, and conversational library queries via MCP) are included out of the box on hosted plans. On self-hosted instances, you must configure and fund your own Cloudflare Workers AI or external model provider directly.
