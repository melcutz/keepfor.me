Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Keepfor.me different from old bookmark apps?

Old bookmark and read-it-later tools were dead ends: you saved a link, forgot it, and it sat trapped in a closed app. Keepfor.me is designed for 2026: anything you save builds a personal context vault that any AI tool (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Copilot) can query whenever you work.

How does my AI connect to my saved context?

Keepfor.me natively supports the open Model Context Protocol (MCP). In Claude Desktop or Cursor, you simply add one line with your Keepfor.me URL and secret token. Your AI automatically retrieves your relevant bookmarks and notes whenever you ask questions.

Can I still use it just as a peaceful reader?

Absolutely! When you do want to read an essay or recipe, Keepfor.me provides a distraction-free reader mode with customizable typography and themes, free from ads and popups.

Is Keepfor.me private and free?

Yes! Keepfor.me is open-source software under the MIT license. There are no tracking scripts, ad networks, or subscription walls. You can use our hosted service or self-host your own instance on Cloudflare for $0/month.
