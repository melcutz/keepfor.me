Natural Search

Find what you remember, even without the title

Can't recall the exact headline? That's completely okay. Search by topic, feeling, or concept.

Search the way human memory actually works

Most bookmark tools only look for exact words in the title. Keepfor.me understands what an article is about. If you remember reading about "morning sunlight habits" or "how to fold sourdough dough," you'll find it right away.

  • Understands Concepts: Connects related ideas even when different words were used.
  • Full-Text Memory: Searches every word inside the article text, not just the headline.
  • Instant Speed: Results appear in a fraction of a second as you type.

🛠️ For Tech Enthusiasts & Developers: Self-Host for Free
Keepfor.me is 100% open-source and runs serverless on Cloudflare's free tier. Click to view technical details.

Modern Serverless Architecture

Built with Python FastAPI running on Cloudflare Workers (Pyodide), D1 SQLite + FTS5 full-text search, R2 object storage for snapshots, and Vectorize for semantic embeddings. Costs $0/mo under Cloudflare's free tier.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Keepfor.me free to use?

Yes, Keepfor.me is open-source software and free. If you host it on Cloudflare, it runs within Cloudflare's generous free tier with zero monthly fees.

Do I need to download an app from the App Store?

No download is required. Keepfor.me is a modern progressive web app. You can add it to your home screen directly from Safari or Chrome, and it behaves just like a native app.

What happens if a website deletes an article I saved?

Your saved copy is completely safe. When you save an article, Keepfor.me stores an exact permanent copy in your library, so you can still read it years later.

Can I organize my articles with tags?

Yes! You can add tags manually or set up automatic tagging rules (for example, tagging anything from your favorite cooking blog as "Recipes").
