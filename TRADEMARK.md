# Trademark Policy

> **Draft pending legal review (H7)**

This document explains the trademark policy for the Keepfor project.

## Marks Covered

The marks covered by this policy include:
- The wordmarks and names **Keepfor** and **Keepfor.me**.
- The Keepfor logo, wordmark, and related brand assets in `static/` (currently `.gitkeep`; any brand imagery added to `static/`) and extension icons (`browser-extension/icon16.png`, `browser-extension/icon48.png`, `browser-extension/icon128.png`).

## Copyright vs. Trademark Rights

Keepfor.me source code is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0-only).

The AGPL grants copyright permissions only, not trademark rights. Software licenses like the AGPL govern copying, modifying, and redistributing code; they do not grant permission to use the project's trademarks, trade names, or logos in ways that could cause confusion.

## Allowed Without Asking

You may do the following without prior written permission:
- **Factual references**: Make truthful, non-misleading factual statements about your software or service (e.g., "compatible with Keepfor.me", "forked from Keepfor.me", "tools for Keepfor").
- **Self-hosting**: Self-host and run Keepfor.me for your own personal use or your organization's internal use under the Keepfor / Keepfor.me project name.
- **Unmodified redistribution**: Distribute exact, unmodified copies of the Keepfor.me source code and binaries with existing marks intact, as permitted under the AGPL license.

## Requires Written Permission

You must obtain prior written permission for:
- Offering a hosted service, SaaS, or commercial product under the name "Keepfor", "Keepfor.me", or any name confusingly similar.
- Using the Keepfor logo or wordmark as part of your own product, service, or company name.
- Any use that implies sponsorship, affiliation, or endorsement by the Keepfor project or its maintainer.

## Modified Forks and Public Services

If you distribute a modified fork of Keepfor.me publicly, or run a modified fork as a service for third parties over a network, you must use a distinct name and distinct branding/logo that is not confusingly similar to Keepfor or Keepfor.me.
