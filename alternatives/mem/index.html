📝 Mem Alternative & Migration Guide

Switching from Mem: Move Your Notes to Keepfor.me

Mem announced it is shutting down. Here is how to export your notes, import your Markdown archive into Keepfor.me, and keep your personal knowledge vault safe from future shutdowns.

Migration Guide | November 2026 Back to Homepage

Why now: Mem is shutting down

On November 16, 2026, Mem announced that it is shutting down its product and service. You can read the official announcement on the Mem blog: https://get.mem.ai/blog/mem-is-shutting-down.

The shutdown follows a two-stage timeline:

  • December 18, 2026: Notes become read-only. After this date, you cannot create or edit notes in Mem.
  • January 15, 2027: Mem shuts down completely, and access to accounts and data ends.

Our recommendation is to export before December 18 while your workspace remains fully active and accessible. This is practical advice for protecting your data, not a Keepfor deadline. Keepfor Cloud will continue to accept Mem export archives after December 18 as long as you have your downloaded ZIP file.

Step 1: Export your notes from Mem

Mem provides an official export tool that bundles your workspace into a Markdown ZIP archive. Follow the official migration guide at https://help.mem.ai/migration/save-a-copy.

On a computer, complete these steps inside Mem:

  1. Open Settings in Mem.
  2. Click Exports in the settings navigation.
  3. Click Start new export.
  4. Select My Computer as the export destination, then click Next.
  5. Review the export options. Leave meeting audio recordings unselected unless you need them (they make the ZIP much larger).
  6. Click Start export.
  7. When the export status shows ready, click Download to save the ZIP file to your computer.

Important: Keep the ZIP zipped. Upload the ZIP as downloaded from Mem, so Keepfor can recognize the Mem layout (notes, collections, attachments, export report).

Step 2: Import your notes into Keepfor

Importing your Mem archive into Keepfor is straightforward:

  1. Log in to your Keepfor account and navigate to Settings.
  2. Scroll down to the Data Management & Backup section (under the Import Bookmarks & Notes card).
  3. Click the file input button to select your downloaded Mem ZIP archive.
  4. Click Upload & Ingest.

Keepfor processes your archive asynchronously through background queues. Both the one-shot summary and the persisted import status panel show real-time progress and final results, including notes imported, duplicates skipped, attachments not imported, and any errors. If you refresh the page, the import status remains available for your review.

What comes across and what does not

Keepfor parses the official Mem export layout to extract your written knowledge cleanly. Here is exactly what transfers and what does not:

Content Type Transferred to Keepfor? Details & Handling
Notes (notes/ folder) Yes Every Markdown note under notes/ is imported as an individual note. Titles are extracted from front matter, the first H1 header, or the filename. Meeting transcripts that are part of the note text are imported in full.
Collections (collections/ folder) Yes (as tags) Mem collection index files are converted into tags on their linked notes. If a note belongs to two collections, it receives both tags. Collection index files themselves are not imported as separate notes.
Source tag (from-mem) Yes Every imported note is automatically tagged from-mem so you can easily identify, filter, or bulk-manage your imported items in the Library.
Attachments (attachments/ folder) Not imported Files in attachments/ (such as images, PDFs, and media) are not imported into Keepfor. Links to attachments in notes will not open in Keepfor; the import summary counts non-imported attachments. Keep your extracted Mem folder on your local drive for access to attachment files.
Meeting audio recordings Not imported Original meeting audio recordings are not stored in Keepfor. Meeting transcripts inside note text are fully imported. Leave audio unselected during export unless you need it.
Export report (export-report.md) Not imported Diagnostic files such as export-report.md are skipped automatically and are not imported as notes.
Duplicate imports Skipped automatically Re-importing the same export skips notes already imported, preventing duplicates.

Archive size limits

Keepfor Cloud accepts a ZIP up to 25 MB per upload. Meeting audio can make the ZIP much larger, so leave it unselected unless you need it; if the ZIP is still over 25 MB it cannot be uploaded in one piece today.

Find or undo your import in Keepfor

If you want to review what was imported or undo the migration entirely, Keepfor gives you full control through existing Library tools:

  • Find imported notes: Open your Library. In the sidebar under Filter by Tag, select the #from-mem tag (or tap #from-mem in the mobile tag bar). This filters your view to show only notes from your Mem migration.
  • Bulk select: Open the filters drawer and click Bulk select, then choose Select all.
  • Move to Trash: In the bulk actions toolbar, click Move to Trash. An Undo prompt is offered right away if you clicked by mistake.
  • Restoring or permanent deletion: Notes in Trash remain recoverable for 30 days. You can restore them anytime from Trash or choose to delete them permanently.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I do not export from Mem before December 18, 2026?

According to Mem's announcement, workspaces become read-only on December 18, 2026. You can still log in and download an export until full shutdown on January 15, 2027. However, we strongly advise exporting before December 18 so you have time to verify your files while Mem remains fully operational.

How are Mem collections handled during import?

Mem stores collections as Markdown index files with links to member notes. Keepfor reads these collections and converts each collection name into a tag on the corresponding notes. If a note belongs to multiple collections, Keepfor applies all relevant tags.

Can I access image and PDF attachments in Keepfor?

No. Keepfor does not import attachment files like images or PDFs from attachments/. Any relative Markdown links to attachments will not display media inside Keepfor. The import summary reports the number of non-imported attachments. We recommend keeping your unzipped Mem export folder on your computer so you retain local copies of all attachments.

What should I do if my Mem export ZIP is larger than 25 MB?

Keepfor Cloud accepts a ZIP up to 25 MB per upload. Meeting audio can make the ZIP much larger, so leave it unselected unless you need it; if the ZIP is still over 25 MB it cannot be uploaded in one piece today.

Can I remove imported Mem notes without deleting my other saves?

Yes. Every note from Mem receives the from-mem tag. In Keepfor, navigate to Library, filter by #from-mem, click Bulk select, select all matching items, and click Move to Trash. Undo is available immediately, and Trash preserves your notes for 30 days before permanent removal.
